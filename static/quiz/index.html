Questions and human response data are from the Thousand-question Spanish general knowledge database by Buades-Sitjar, Boada, Guasch, Ferré, Hinojosa, Brysbaert & Duñabeitia, published in Psicológica 42 (2021), 109–119 and used under CC BY 4.0. Its human percentages come from participants in Spain, so some questions may be easier or harder for you depending on where you live. A few category names had their spelling fixed.

nimble is an open-weights AI based on Qwen 3.5-9B. It's a decision model: an AI designed to pick from a given set of options rather than give freeform text answers. Its answers were recorded in advance; this page doesn't contact any AI.
